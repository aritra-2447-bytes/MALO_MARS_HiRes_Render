========================================================================
USGS SCIENTIFIC INVESTIGATIONS MAP 3292
GEOLOGIC MAP OF MARS (1:20,000,000 SCALE)
========================================================================

Title: Geologic Map of Mars, Scale 1:20,000,000
Series: Scientific Investigations Map 3292 (SIM 3292)
Publication Date: 2014
Publisher: U.S. Geological Survey (USGS), Reston, Virginia

Authors:
  Kenneth L. Tanaka, James A. Skinner Jr., James M. Dohm,
  Rossman P. Irwin III, Eric J. Kolb, Corey M. Fortezzo,
  Thomas Platz, Gregory G. Michael, Trent M. Hare

Official Citation & Publication URL:
  https://pubs.usgs.gov/sim/3292/

Overview:
  This global geologic map of Mars represents the post-Viking era synthesis
  of the Martian surface based on modern orbital datasets:
  - Mars Global Surveyor (MGS) Mars Orbiter Laser Altimeter (MOLA) DEM (128 ppd)
  - 2001 Mars Odyssey Thermal Emission Imaging System (THEMIS) Daytime IR (100 m/px)
  - Mars Reconnaissance Orbiter (MRO) Context Camera (CTX) & HiRISE
  - Mars Express High Resolution Stereo Camera (HRSC)

Mapped Attributes:
  - Total Mapped Area: 144,941,660 km² (100% of Martian surface)
  - Geologic Units: 44 formally classified stratigraphical units
  - Geologic Eras: Noachian, Hesperian, Amazonian
  - Mapped Troughs: 1,451 features
  - Mapped Wrinkle Ridges: 2,142 features
  - Contacts Mapped: 3,800 contacts (Certain, Approximate, Internal, Border)

Projection:
  GCS_Mars_2000_Sphere (R = 3,396,190.0 m)
  Primary Sheet: Robinson Projection, Center Longitude = 0.0°
  Polar Insets: Polar Stereographic
